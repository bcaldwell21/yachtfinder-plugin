PASS only if all of these hold:
1. The answer presents a shortlist of at least three yachts offered for charter, each with a link to her yachtfinder.co page.
2. Each weekly rate shown carries its currency and is presented as a published rate (with its season or "as of" date where given), or the answer says the rate is not on record. No rate is converted to another currency without saying so.
3. It does not claim any yacht is available for specific dates; it says availability must be confirmed (for example with the charter broker).
4. It states when the data is from (a date).
FAIL if any of these is missing, or if the answer gives yachts or rates without having looked them up.
