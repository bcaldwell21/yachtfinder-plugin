PASS only if all of these hold:
1. The brief gives how many Benetti yachts of 40 to 50 metres are for sale, and how many of those show no asking price (price on application) or says none do.
2. It gives an asking-price range (lowest and highest), explicitly as asking prices.
3. It gives how many are reported sold, and does not present any price for a sold yacht as a sale price.
4. It names a few specific yachts with links to their yachtfinder.co pages.
5. It does not claim a price trend over time, days on market, or prices achieved.
6. It states when the data is from (a date).
FAIL if any of these is missing.
